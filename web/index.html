The whole protocol

Three moves, no intermediary reading your number.

The auction is a shielded address plus a deadline in block height. Bids are payments. Clearing is a pure function. Nothing is hidden from the verifier — only from the market.

  1. 01

    Open

    The seller publishes a fresh shielded address, a reserve, a clearing rule, and a deadline height. The dashboard reveals only a hash of the viewing key it will disclose later.

    params_hash committed at open

  2. 02

    Bid

    A bidder sends ZEC from any ZIP-321 wallet with a memo holding a fresh shielded refund address. The amount never leaves the ciphertext. Transparent funding is rejected.

    memo ≤ 512 bytes · encrypted to the auction

  3. 03

    Settle

    After the deadline the auctioneer clears, publishes a root and a leaf table, refunds every loser in one batch, and reveals the UFVK so anyone can rescan and recompute.

    one batch · one height · inclusion proof per bid

            Disclosures
            
        
            What this does not protect you from.
            
        
            
          Privacy is a precondition for the market to exist, not a marketing claim. These are the
          leaks and trust assumptions we know about, published rather than discovered later.
        
            
      
            
      
            
        
            
          
            Custody is trusted
            
          
            
            Bids sit in an account whose spending key the auctioneer holds. Zcash has no escrow
            contract and no timelock on the shielded pool.
          
            
        
            
        
            
          
            Refunds are auditable, not guaranteed
            
          
            
            A pre-committed OVK can prove that a refund is missing. It cannot prevent it. Detection
            matters only behind a bond, a reputation, or a legal counterparty.
          
            
        
            
        
            
          
            Inclusion proofs prove inclusion
            
          
            
            An omitted bid is a bid with no object to point at. Detectable from chain data in public
            tenders; otherwise only if the bidder discloses their own bid.
          
            
        
            
        
            
          
            Delivery ends the privacy
            
          
            
            Handing over a physical lot, an NFT, or a contract re-identifies the winner. Privacy
            stops at settlement, and this page says so before you bid.
